Aboutコラボレーションについて

Beloved Japanese IP, officially licensed.

HANAJIRUSHI holds official licences for several of Japan's best-known IP, with end-to-end capability to develop collaboration products — from formula to packaging and gift sets.

Licensors audit their partners rigorously on quality, finance and compliance. Holding these licences is itself a third-party endorsement of how we work.
